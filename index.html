THE RULES

How to play

Every pie is created from the actual colors in a professional sports team's logo. The logo stays hidden until your game ends.

01
Study the colors

The size of each slice shows its share of the logo's visible pixels. Transparent background pixels don't count.

02
Name that team

Search all teams, or filter to the NFL, NHL, NBA, or MLB. You have six guesses per puzzle.

03
Get closer

Wrong guesses reveal new clues. Solve a daily puzzle or practice as much as you like in Unlimited.
